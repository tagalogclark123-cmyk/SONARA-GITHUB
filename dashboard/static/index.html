        Help

        How to read the dashboard

        What the status means

        • NormalNo unusual sounds heard recently.
        • WatchSome unusual sounds. Listen to them and look at the birds when you can.
        • Check birdsMany unusual sounds close together. Check the birds in this pen.
        • No dataThe sensor stopped sending sound. Check the sensor, its power and its distance to the receiver.

        What SONARA can and cannot do

        • It listens for coughing, sneezing and other breathing-related sounds, and tells you which pen they came from.
        • It cannot tell which disease the birds have. Only a veterinarian can.
        • Quiet or resting birds make little sound, so a sick bird may not be heard.
        • It does not decide on medicine or treatment. If your birds look sick, contact a veterinarian.
